Setup redesign prototypes

Three clickable takes on the setup screen. They use sample stops and departures, and nothing is saved.

Recommended

A · Layout first

  • Pick Single, Two stops, or Buses and trains first
  • Each side gets its own stop search, heading and routes
  • Live preview beside the form
  • Footer says exactly what's missing and jumps to it

B · Live editor

  • The board is the editor: click a side to set its stop
  • Click a route pill to change its colour
  • Settings panel on the right
  • The same view opens from the board's gear button

C · Tabbed

  • Board, Display and Connection tabs
  • Layout is a segmented control
  • Least change from today's screen
  • Advanced fields live under Connection

What all three share

  • No build step: one Open board button, and changes save automatically
  • Timetable and key boxes become one status line
  • The key setting is a plain three-way choice: built-in, your own, or no key
  • Route filters are clickable pills; colours have pickers instead of a text format
  • Anything blocking the board is shown next to the field that needs it
  • No preset dropdown
